## Channel adaptation
- LinkedIn: publish one creative and one text caption weekly; call to action links to campaign page only after it is deployed and verified.
- Partner introductions: include a single image and short relevant paragraph; customize to the clinic's priorities and country/time zone.
- Website: direct traffic to `/beyond-the-standard/` when published, then onward to `/request-demo/`.
- U.S. / Africa: use the same core visual identity, but verify local examples and regulations and avoid implying live availability in unsupported markets.
- Conference/print: use the vector creative with bleed/sizing handled during production.

## Performance worksheet
For each placement record: date, region, channel, creative ID, landing-page visits (aggregate, privacy reviewed), legitimate inquiry count, qualified inquiry count, demos completed, opportunity status, and paid cost if separately approved. Never record patient or medical information in marketing analytics.

## Final publishing review
- Clinical capability claims and geography are verified.
- Existing site wordmark is used; the exploratory blue logo icon in the moodboard is not approved.
- Text fits at full resolution and in the platform's mobile crops.
- Landing page and all CTA destinations are live and accessible.
- No personal health information in creatives, ad targeting, or analytics.
- No mail sent and no spending without explicit approval.
