## Film production rules
- 16:9 primary, crop-safe for 1:1 and 9:16; closed captions on all versions.
- Only fictional synthetic/consented imagery and invented records. No identifiable patient data.
- No unverified statements such as HIPAA-certified, real-time interoperability, improved clinical outcomes, or fully deployed modules.
- Score: composed original music or properly licensed; no recognizable commercial melody.
- Use the real `mmed-wordmark.svg` from the site, or its exact vector lettering.
- CTA directs to `https://mmedsystems.com/request-demo/` and preserves invitation-only process.
- No paid distribution without explicit approval.

## Campaign variants

## QA and release gates
- Healthcare claim/legal review.
- Accessibility, captions, mobile crop check.
- Test every website CTA.
- Confirm creative usage rights and remove unintended PHI.
- Verify site experience and actual deployment before using ads.
- Add privacy-respecting attribution only after review. No third-party ad tracking pixels by default.

## Suggested organic pilot
Run four variant messages on company LinkedIn, industry outreach and partner-facing materials. Record meaningful engagement and qualified private-demo requests. Paid placements require a separately approved budget.
