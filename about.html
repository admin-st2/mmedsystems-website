ABOUT MMED SYSTEMS

Connected information. Coordinated care.

Meet MMeD Systems, a healthcare technology company developing practical, connected software for clinical teams and healthcare operators.

We believe technology should make healthcare work better.

MMeD Systems is developing an integrated healthcare technology platform that connects clinical records, communication, and operational workflows.

Our focus is the needs of the organizations and people doing the work—not simply adding more functionality to an already complicated experience. We intend to design for adaptability across different countries, care settings, and organizational sizes.

Where we are today

MMeD is an early-stage platform in active design and development. We are gathering input from healthcare professionals to shape requirements and validate priorities.
