ABOUT MMED SYSTEMS

A long-term vision for more connected care.

MMEd Systems is a healthcare technology company developing connected digital solutions for healthcare providers. Our vision is to connect clinical information, enterprise operations, supply chain processes and financial accountability through modular healthcare software that can grow with facilities of different sizes.

Our platform is currently in development. We welcome conversations with organizations interested in helping shape it.
