FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

The platform

Understand the product and the organizations it is intended to support.

What does MMeD Systems do?

MMeD Systems develops configurable electronic medical records (EMR/EHR) and healthcare management software. Its goal is to connect patient information, clinical tasks, and business operations. Availability of specific features and integrations must be confirmed for each implementation. Explore the platform.

Who is the software designed for?

MMeD is designed for healthcare organizations and care teams with different care models, team structures, and operational needs. Explore our Who We Serve directory for dedicated sector workflows and implementation questions.

What is the difference between EMR and EHR software?

An electronic medical record (EMR) commonly describes a digital patient record within a practice or organization. An electronic health record (EHR) is often used for information that supports coordination across care settings. The terms overlap; when selecting a system, ask how information is accessed, secured, transferred, and used in practice.

What workflows is MMeD developing?

Development areas include registration, appointments, nursing and provider notes, diagnostic orders, results, medication workflows, referrals, billing, inventory, and reporting. These are design and demonstration priorities, not a guarantee that every module is commercially available. See Clinical Care and Business Operations.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Demonstrations and implementation

Find out what happens before a system is deployed.

How do I request a demonstration?

Visit Request a Demo to schedule a private demonstration or compose a business inquiry by email. You can also view all meeting options. Booking reserves a meeting time only. Private demo access requires MMeD approval and NDA acceptance, with a separate secure invitation. Please do not submit patient information.

What will a walkthrough show?

An approved walkthrough may cover representative registration, appointments, documentation, laboratory, pharmacy, and billing workflows using fictional data. The exact agenda and demonstrated capabilities depend on the current development version and your organization's needs. We distinguish functioning features from planned ones.

Can I upload real patient records into the demo?

No. The demonstration is intended for fictional test information only. Do not upload, enter, or email protected health information (PHI), actual patient records, passwords, or sensitive clinical data through the demo or public inquiry pages.

How would migration and onboarding work?

A potential migration requires assessment of your current system's exports, data quality, patient identification, clinical safety, permissions, testing, and cutover. Training, configuration, implementation schedules, and onboarding fees would be scoped individually; a fixed timeline or one-click migration is not promised.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Security, privacy and interoperability

Understand the safeguards being designed and what requires verification.

Is MMeD HIPAA-certified or guaranteed compliant?

MMeD does not claim a government-issued HIPAA product certification or a blanket compliance guarantee. A regulated U.S. deployment requires appropriate risk assessment, security practices, organizational procedures and agreements, including a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) where applicable. Read our security approach.

How will staff access and patient information be protected?

Role-based access, authorization, audit records, and data protection are foundational design priorities. Actual security controls and testing evidence must be evaluated for the specific environment. A fictional-data demo is not proof of production compliance.

Does MMeD integrate with laboratory, pharmacy or billing providers?

Integration planning includes clinical and operational systems. Actual connections depend on each vendor's interfaces, permissions, technical compatibility, security, and testing. We do not claim a particular vendor integration is certified or production-ready unless it is verified. Learn about integrations.

Does MMeD support HL7 or FHIR?

HL7 and FHIR are healthcare data exchange standards considered in interoperability planning. Support for any specific message, resource, version, use case, or external connection must be verified before it becomes an implementation commitment.

Pricing, availability and contacting MMeD

Commercial and international questions, answered directly.

How much does MMeD Systems cost?

Pricing is tailored to each organization. Contact us for an itemized proposal outlining the software, implementation, integrations, training, and support relevant to your requirements. Costs and included services are discussed and confirmed before an agreement. Learn about organization-specific pricing or request an itemized proposal.

Can MMeD support multiple clinics or locations?

Multi-location workflows, facility-level permissions, referrals, and reporting are part of the design direction. Requirements and readiness must be evaluated for the organization and intended scale. Explore care networks.

Which countries does MMeD work with?

MMeD welcomes conversations with providers in the United States, Uganda, other East African countries, wider Africa, and internationally. Interest in a market does not establish a local office, live customer deployment, or regulatory authorization there. Contact us to discuss your country-specific requirements.

How can I contact MMeD about my organization?

Visit Contact for business inquiry options, or Request a Demo if you are evaluating the platform. Include your organization type, country, and general priorities, but no patient-identifying information.
