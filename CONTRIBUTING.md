# Contributions and intellectual property

This repository is maintained as the public MMEd Systems website, with
proprietary rights reserved in eligible original materials.

## Before proposing a contribution

Unsolicited contributions are not currently accepted. Do not submit code,
designs, documentation, or other copyrightable materials for inclusion until
the maintainer has expressly approved the work and applicable written
contractor/contributor terms have been executed.

Contributors must disclose the origin of any material they propose to add,
including:
- original human-authored work and their right to contribute it;
- AI-assisted or AI-generated content and meaningful human revisions;
- third-party software, libraries, images, fonts, and licenses;
- pre-existing employer, client, or contractor obligations.

**A pull request, commit, or suggestion alone does not transfer ownership.**
Written assignments and other legal instruments will be arranged where
appropriate before work is accepted.

For permission or contribution inquiries: info@mmedsystems.com

Do not submit patient data, credentials, confidential healthcare software,
or undisclosed security issues to this public repository.
