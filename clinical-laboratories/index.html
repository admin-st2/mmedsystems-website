A QUESTION WORTH ASKING

Can MMeD connect directly to our laboratory equipment?

That depends on the equipment and available interfaces. Device compatibility, message formats, validation responsibilities, and result-handling requirements must be reviewed before any connection is promised.

This page describes MMeD’s design and implementation approach, not a claim that every workflow is available in production.
