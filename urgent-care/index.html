Urgent Care and Walk-In Clinics

Bring clarity to a changing patient queue.

Urgent care and walk-in services need an organized approach to arrivals, clinical assessment, documentation, and discharge. MMeD Systems considers how teams can keep track of each encounter while responsibilities move between reception, nursing, providers, and administrative staff.

WORKING WITH MMED

How MMeD works
with your team.

We map the visit from arrival through disposition, including patient identification, queue status, clinical assessments, orders, results, and discharge instructions. The evaluation also considers how completed services connect with charge capture and payment processes.

The aim is a clear view of what has happened, what remains outstanding, and who is responsible next.

A QUESTION WORTH ASKING

Can scheduled appointments and walk-ins be considered together?

Yes. We would review how both encounter types should appear in the clinic’s workflow, including status changes, staff responsibilities, and exceptions. Specific functionality would be confirmed during evaluation.

This page describes MMeD’s design and implementation approach, not a claim that every workflow is available in production.
