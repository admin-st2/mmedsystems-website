QUESTIONS

A few things
worth knowing.

Is there a one-time setup or onboarding fee?+

The onboarding fee is to be confirmed. The proposal to waive it on annual plans remains under commercial review; no waiver is promised yet.

Are contracts required, and how does cancellation work?+

Contract length, renewal terms and cancellation policy have not been finalized. We will publish them before accepting orders.

What does onboarding include?+

A scoped implementation conversation would cover configuration, data preparation, staff roles, training, and rollout responsibilities. The exact service level and timeline will be confirmed.

Can you migrate data from our existing EHR?+

Migration depends on access to the source system, export formats, data quality, integrations and regulatory requirements. Scope and cost require assessment.

How is support provided?+

The proposed plans distinguish standard, priority and dedicated support; response times and coverage hours will be defined in the final offer and applicable SLA.

Do prices include AI notes or extra modules?+

No add-on price has been confirmed. AI notes, extra modules and additional user licenses remain under review.

Is there a discount for 10 or more providers?+

Volume discount eligibility is planned for discussion, but the percentage has not yet been finalized.

Is the platform HIPAA-certified?+

No official HIPAA product certification is claimed. Security safeguards, Business Associate Agreements and production readiness must be verified for the applicable implementation.
