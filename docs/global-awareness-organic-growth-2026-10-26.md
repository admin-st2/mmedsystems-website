# MMeD Systems | Global awareness and organic search plan
**Site:** https://mmedsystems.com/  
**Release:** October 26, 2026  
**Scope:** Public corporate website only; no clinical-demo changes or lead-backend activation.

## Why these changes
- Homepage was missing an obvious answer to "where do you work / where do you serve?"
- Many audience and platform pages were brief and relied on near-identical general value propositions.
- Helpful clinical workflow and buyer-evaluation detail was not prominent.
- Resources page described future content rather than giving visitors useful material immediately.
- Sitewide navigation, mobile menu and footer did not link to any geographic/service-market overview.

## Work in this release
- New canonical, internally linked **/where-we-serve/** page: United States; Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, Rwanda and East African engagement priority; wider African markets; other international requests.
- Clearly **distinguishes target and discussion markets from verified installed clients, physical offices, compliance certificates, or guaranteed local availability**.
- Visible homepage regional section, desktop and mobile "Where We Serve" navigation, footer link on all 26 indexable public pages.
- Expanded authentic buyer guidance for private clinics, medical centers, hospitals, solutions, platforms, integrated workflows, operational finance, About and Resources.
- Original resources buyer checklist on documentation, workflows, privacy, payment operations, interfaces, and local requirements.
- Updated SEO metadata, 26-url HTTPS sitemap, accurate lastmod values for substantive edits only.
- White and navy editorial style, Times New Roman typography, responsive layout and one homepage primary demo CTA preserved.

## First 30 days after release
1. Free Google Search Console: check sitemap and inspect /where-we-serve/, /, /solutions/, /private-clinics/, /medical-centers/, /hospitals/, /resources/. Use Google's free interface; paid GSC Wizard is NOT required.
2. Verify Bing Webmaster ownership, IndexNow key and sitemap; Google and Bing may take time to discover new content.
3. Track clicks, impressions and nonbrand queries by query, page and country; specifically look for software searches associated with EMR/EHR, clinic management, hospital management, patient records, pharmacy/laboratory, interoperability and billing.
4. Review and measure real business inquiry requests; avoid passing personal information to analytics. The current form still requires the visitor to send an email.
5. Promote the region page organically through a company-controlled LinkedIn profile and professional networks, with factual copy and no purchased traffic or fabricated testimonial.

## 30–90-day content/awareness opportunities (require additional source research)
- Develop genuine country-specific briefs **only** where provider interviews, published regulations and local workflows support distinct content: begin Uganda/U.S., then consider Kenya/Tanzania/Rwanda and other verified markets. A list of cloned country doorway pages is not a substitute.
- Publish plain-language educational articles: "Evaluating an EMR in a small clinic", "How medical-center departments share orders and results", "Clinical and administrative role separation", "ERP/SCM considerations for hospitals", and "How to prepare for EHR data migration".
- Collaborate with healthcare business associations, legitimate industry publications, clinical management communities, and partners. Seek earned mentions/backlinks; never buy manipulative link networks.
- Establish a verifiable company "About" profile and qualified editorial review for healthcare/business articles. Publish only confirmed implementations or named clients with written permission.
- Translate regional articles only when there is a well-supported local language audience, accurate translation/review and dedicated canonical URLs. Use hreflang only for real language/region variants, not for one English global page.
- Establish monthly reporting: indexed canonical URLs, relevant impressions/clicks, qualified inquiries, approved demonstration invitations, and country-specific demand. Traffic is not guaranteed by metadata changes.

## Search Console reporting limitation
GSC Wizard's connected account trial is over and currently returns `payment_required`. We did not open a paid subscription. Use the first-party free Google Search Console and free Bing Webmaster tools, or authorize new connections if appropriate, before reporting newly verified performance numbers. Prior indexing metrics are historical snapshots.

- Verify desktop/mobile navigation and accessible H1/metadata, self canonicals, resource links, sitemap count, no private-demo URL leaked, and single primary CTA on homepage.
- Github Actions static-site and browser-smoke jobs must pass before merge; verify live new route after Github Pages propagation.
