with your team.

A QUESTION WORTH ASKING

Can different programs have different workflows?

That should be addressed explicitly during planning. Some information may need to be shared, while documentation, staff responsibilities, and access requirements may differ by service or program.

This page describes MMeD’s design and implementation approach, not a claim that every workflow is available in production.
