PRACTICAL CONSIDERATIONS

What changes at hospital scale.

Hospital systems must support complex departmental responsibilities, clinical safety, and coordination while keeping business controls distinct from direct patient care.

  • Registration, emergency and inpatient encounters, clinical notes, medication administration, and handoffs across nursing and provider teams.
  • Laboratory and imaging orders, results routing, pharmacy safety workflows, referrals, discharge, and follow-up.
  • Utilization review, revenue cycle, billing reconciliation, inventory, procurement and supply-chain oversight as requirements grow.
  • Identity, authorization, interoperability, training, monitoring and local regulatory review before any production deployment.
