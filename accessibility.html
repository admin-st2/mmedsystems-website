ACCESSIBILITY

Healthcare technology should be accessible.

We aim to make MMeD's public website navigable and understandable for as many visitors as possible.

Our approach

We use semantic headings, keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus indicators, responsive layouts, and readable text contrast. Our design and engineering process is ongoing, and we have not claimed an independent accessibility certification or formal audit.

Feedback or assistance

If you encounter a barrier while using the website, describe the page and issue to contact@mmedsystems.com. We welcome feedback and will use it to guide improvements.
