Privacy Policy

How personal information is handled when you visit or contact MMeD Systems.

This policy describes this public informational website. Product applications and future demonstrations may have separate privacy terms.

Information you choose to share

You may contact us at an MMeD business email address or prepare an email using our demo inquiry form. Your email may include your name, contact details, organization, location, and information you volunteer about your needs. Please do not include patient records or sensitive health information.

How this website works

This version of the website is statically hosted. It does not submit inquiry form data to an MMeD web server, and we have not installed analytics or advertising cookies in this build. The demo form prepares an email draft in your email application. Sending it is your decision. The website hosting provider may maintain standard technical logs.

How we use inquiries

We use correspondence to respond to questions, consider partnerships, understand organizational needs, and plan product discussions. We do not sell inquiry data to advertisers. Email services and hosting providers may process information to provide their services.

Retention and requests

We retain business correspondence as needed for legitimate operational purposes and applicable obligations. To request access, correction, or deletion where applicable, contact info@mmedsystems.com.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the website and products develop. For privacy inquiries, write to info@mmedsystems.com. Last updated October 2026.
